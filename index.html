 I'm Luka Vivi Starr Alice, also known as different variations of the nicknames "StarRflowerR" or "Celest1nee", but you can just call me Luka. I've often introduced myself as a "serial doer and liker of things" due to the variety of interests I have, and a much greater interest that I have in actually indulging myself in said interests or talking about them, rather than introducing myself or summarizing what I like to do.

 However, there a few thing you might want to know about me, so here goes a lazy little list:

  • Once upon a time, I was an undergraduate (BA/BEng.) Computer Science student! I almost finished it, and now I am now an undergraduate (BA/BEng.) Biomedical Engineering student with a plan on continuing my education in the far future until I either: a) become enlisted as a PhD student on the front of physics or something physics-adjacent, or b) have finished both my present and unfinished degrees and found my way back into the wicked Big Conputer industry.
  • I have participated in an art exhibition project at a queer library & local community space "Biblioteka Azyl" (Azyl Library), the project itself concerning the experiences of trans & non-binary artists.
  • I have a 98.5% legit vanilla Minecraft survival world that I've been working on since 26th September 2019! (and it's very much ongoing). My MC world's official name is New World 4. Nearing its 6 years old mark, it has undergone some changes that made it "questionably no longer vanilla" and "even less legit" (and to some extent "no longer singleplayer", but technically not quite SMP either"; it's got an interesting history!).
  • I have a casual "STEM+" research project called "ω Notes" (omega Notes) that I work on from time to time. Eventually I plan to port it to a website format under the name of "ψ Notes" (psi Notes). It's not–and never will be–a fully open, public or "formally published" project due to intellectual property concerns and the fact that ultimately it is just my personal collection of highly structured and formatted reference notes.
  • I am a part of a collective behind a strange, long-term, narrative art project under the names "Continuum Reverie" and "Pathway to Eden". The collective itself too has two names: "TransOrder of Eden" and "Solstice Initiative". It may sound like a shady cult, but our individual motives, factual positions in everyday life in society, and most importantly - wallets, serve to suggest otherwise. Once we finally manage to get something out there, feel free to check it out!

And that would be all! Have fun exploring my silly little website!
